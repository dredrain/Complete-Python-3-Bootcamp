What it checks, per cable

Four checks, every conductor, with automatic up-sizing until all pass.

① Design current & capacity

Ib for 1- or 3-phase loads, required Iz′ = Ib / (Ca·Cg), and the smallest standard size that meets it.

② Voltage drop

ΔU = k·Ib·L·(R·cosφ + X·sinφ) at operating temperature; steps up if it exceeds your per-cable limit.

③ Short-circuit withstand

Adiabatic check S ≥ Iscc·√t / k (IEC 60364-4-43); steps up until the cable survives the fault.

④ Materials & methods

Copper & aluminium, XLPE & PVC, installation methods B1 / C / E / F.

FAQ

Is this a certified design tool?

No. CableCalc is an engineering aid that automates the routine calculation. Results must be verified against the applicable standard edition and the cable manufacturer's datasheet by a qualified engineer before use.

Which standards does it use?

Ampacity and correction factors follow IEC 60364-5-52; the short-circuit check uses the adiabatic method with IEC 60364-4-43 material constants. The copper/XLPE ampacities are a representative subset of the standard; PVC and aluminium are derived by documented derating factors you can replace with tabulated values.

Do I need to know Python?
